About SoleCraft

Born from a frustration with ordinary shoes

We're a small, ambitious team of footwear obsessives, designers, and material scientists who got tired of settling — for ill-fitting sizes, uninspired designs, and fast-fashion quality that falls apart.

SoleCraft is our answer. We're building a platform and manufacturing pipeline that makes fully custom footwear accessible to anyone — not just the ultra-luxury buyer.

We're currently in early development and actively taking pre-bookings from our first community of believers. If you've ever wanted a pair of shoes made exactly for you, this is your moment.

How It Works

From your imagination
to your doorstep

Our process is designed to be simple, transparent, and collaborative. You're not just buying shoes — you're co-creating them. Here's how the SoleCraft experience works once we officially launch.

01🎨

Design Your Pair

Use our design configurator to choose your silhouette, upper material, lining, sole type, colorways, and custom details.

02📐

Submit Measurements

Send us your exact foot measurements using our guided at-home measurement kit. No generic sizing.

03🔨

Crafting Begins

Our skilled artisans construct your pair from scratch using the materials and specifications you selected.

04✅

Quality Review

Before your shoes leave our workshop, they go through rigorous quality inspection. We send photos for your approval.

05📦

Delivered to You

Your custom pair arrives in thoughtful packaging with care instructions and a profile card documenting every detail.

Got Questions?

Frequently
Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SoleCraft, the pre-booking process, and what to expect when we launch.

What does pre-booking mean? Am I paying now?

+

Pre-booking reserves your spot in our first production run. No payment is required at this stage. You're simply expressing interest and securing priority access. When we're ready to begin design consultations, you'll hear from us first.

Can I cancel my pre-booking?

+

Absolutely. Since no payment is taken at pre-booking, you can withdraw at any time before your design session begins. Just drop us an email and we'll remove you from the list — no questions asked.

How many pre-booking slots are available?

+

We're limiting our first production cohort to 200 pairs. This allows us to give each customer the attention and quality assurance they deserve in our first run. Once those slots fill up, we'll open a waitlist for the second cohort.

What shoe types will be available at launch?

+

For our first production run, we'll offer four core categories: Custom Sneakers, Formal Oxford-style shoes, Chelsea Boots, and Loafers/Mules. Each category will have multiple silhouette options and material/color combinations.

What's the price range for custom SoleCraft shoes?

+

Pricing will depend on the materials you select and the complexity of the design. Our target range is ₹8,000–₹28,000 (approximately $100–$350 USD) for standard configurations. Premium and exotic materials will carry additional costs. Founding members will receive the best rates we ever offer.

How do I get my measurements to you?

+

We'll send you a detailed measurement guide once your design session is booked. It's a simple at-home process — you'll need a sheet of paper, a pencil, and a tape measure. For customers in select cities, we'll offer in-person measurement appointments as well.

How long does it take to make a pair?

+

Our target production time is 4–6 weeks from design approval to delivery. This includes material sourcing, construction, quality review, and shipping. We'll provide real-time updates throughout the process.

Do you ship internationally?

+

We plan to ship internationally from launch. Initial shipping partners will cover India, the US, UK, EU, UAE, and Australia. If your country isn't listed, reach out — we'll do our best to find a solution.

What if the shoes don't fit when they arrive?

+

We take measurements seriously and our design consultation process is thorough. In the rare case of a fit issue due to our error, we'll remake the pair at no extra charge. We'll work out a detailed fit guarantee policy before launch.

Are you really a startup? When did you start?

+

Yes — SoleCraft was founded in 2025 and is currently in pre-launch development. We're a team of five building out our platform, supply chain, and artisan network. We expect to begin first deliveries in late 2025 or early 2026.

Are you looking for investors or partners?

+

We're currently in early conversations with select angel investors. If you're interested in the footwear space and believe in what we're building, we'd love to talk. Reach out via our Contact page with a brief introduction.

Where are the shoes actually made?

+

Our initial manufacturing partners are based in India, with artisans who have decades of experience in bespoke footwear. We're building relationships with additional workshops in Portugal and Italy for premium and formal lines.
